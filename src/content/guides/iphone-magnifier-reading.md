Small print on a medicine bottle, a menu, or a product label can be difficult to read. The good news is that your iPhone has a built-in **iPhone Magnifier** that can make nearby text and objects appear larger on the screen.

Magnifier works like a digital magnifying glass. You point your camera at something, adjust the view, and read it on your display. You don't need to install an extra app.

## Step 1: Find Magnifier on your iPhone

Magnifier is an Apple accessibility feature. It may already be available on your iPhone, even if you have never used it.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Accessibility**.
3. Scroll to the **Vision** section and tap **Magnifier**.
4. Turn on **Magnifier** if it is switched off.

You can also look for the **Magnifier** app on your Home Screen or in the App Library. If you cannot find it, swipe down on the Home Screen to open Search, type “Magnifier,” and tap the app if it appears.

[SCREENSHOT: iPhone Settings Accessibility page showing Magnifier]

> **Good to know:** Magnifier is separate from the normal Camera app. It is designed to help you examine nearby details, with controls for enlarging and adjusting the view.

## Step 2: Open Magnifier

Once Magnifier is enabled, you can open it just like any other app.

1. Find and tap the **Magnifier** app icon.
2. If prompted, allow the app to access the camera. It needs the camera to show a magnified live view.
3. Point the back of your iPhone toward the text or object you want to examine.
4. Hold the phone steady and move it closer or farther away until the item is clear.

[SCREENSHOT: Magnifier app open with a live view of small printed text]

If the image looks blurry, try adjusting the distance slightly. Good lighting can also make small print easier to see.

> **Tip:** Rest your elbows on a table or another steady surface if your hands are moving. A steadier phone makes the magnified image easier to read.

## Step 3: Make the image larger or smaller

Magnifier includes an on-screen zoom control. Use it to choose how much enlargement you need.

1. With Magnifier open, find the zoom slider near the bottom of the screen.
2. Drag the slider to the right to enlarge the view.
3. Drag it to the left to reduce the enlargement.
4. Adjust the position of your iPhone to bring the words or details into the center of the screen.

[SCREENSHOT: Magnifier screen with the zoom slider highlighted]

Very high magnification can make the image look less clear, especially if the phone is moving or the lighting is poor. Use the lowest zoom level that lets you read comfortably.

## Step 4: Improve the view with brightness and contrast

If the text is still difficult to make out, try adjusting the image. Magnifier offers controls that can help make details easier to see.

1. In Magnifier, tap the **Controls** button if the controls are not already visible. The layout can vary by iOS version.
2. Look for the brightness and contrast controls.
3. Adjust brightness to make the image lighter or darker.
4. Adjust contrast to make differences between light and dark areas more noticeable.
5. Return to the live view and see whether the text is easier to read.

[SCREENSHOT: Magnifier controls showing brightness and contrast adjustments]

Some versions of Magnifier also offer filters or other image adjustments. Their names and positions can vary. If you do not see a control described here, check the controls shown on your own screen rather than searching for a setting that may not be available on your version.

> **Careful:** Magnifier can help you inspect small print, but don't rely on it alone for important medical or safety information if the wording is unclear. Ask someone to check the label or use another reliable way to read it.

## Step 5: Freeze the image to read it more easily

Sometimes it is easier to look at a still image than to hold your phone over an object. Magnifier lets you freeze the current view.

1. Point your iPhone at the text or object.
2. Adjust the zoom until the detail is large enough.
3. Tap the **Freeze Frame** button, usually shown as a shutter-style control.
4. Read the still image on the screen.
5. Tap the control to return to the live camera view, or use the on-screen option to take another view if shown.

[SCREENSHOT: Magnifier app displaying a frozen close-up of printed text]

The exact control layout can differ between iPhone models and iOS versions. If you cannot identify the Freeze Frame control, look at the buttons displayed in Magnifier or search for “Magnifier” in **Settings**.

## Step 6: Open Magnifier quickly when you need it

If you use Magnifier often, you can make it easier to open without searching for the app each time.

One option is to add it to Control Center:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Control Center**.
3. Look through the available controls for **Magnifier**.
4. Tap the add control if Magnifier is not already included.
5. Open Control Center and tap the Magnifier control when you need it.

[SCREENSHOT: iPhone Control Center customization screen with Magnifier available to add]

You can also use the Accessibility Shortcut if you prefer. Open **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Accessibility Shortcut**, then select **Magnifier** if it is listed. On supported iPhones, pressing the side button three times opens the selected shortcut. iPhones with a Home button use a triple-click of the Home button instead. If you have several shortcuts selected, a menu may appear so you can choose one.

> **Tip:** Choose the access method that feels easiest to remember. Adding Magnifier to Control Center is convenient if you already use Control Center for other settings.

## Troubleshooting: If Magnifier isn't working

Try these simple checks if you cannot open Magnifier or the picture is unclear.

- **You can't find the app:** Swipe down on the Home Screen and search for “Magnifier.” You can also check **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Magnifier**.
- **The screen is black:** Check whether Magnifier has permission to use the camera. If a permission message appears, follow the prompt.
- **The image is blurry:** Clean the camera lens with a soft, clean cloth, improve the lighting, and move the phone slightly closer or farther away.
- **The text is too small:** Increase the zoom gradually. If the image becomes unclear, reduce zoom and adjust the phone's distance.
- **Your controls look different:** Apple can change the layout between iOS versions. Use the controls visible in your Magnifier app, and search for “Magnifier” in Settings if you need help finding its options.

## A handy tool that's already on your iPhone

Magnifier can make small print and everyday details easier to examine, whether you're reading a label or looking closely at an object. Add it to Control Center or your Accessibility Shortcut, and it will be easier to reach whenever you need it.
