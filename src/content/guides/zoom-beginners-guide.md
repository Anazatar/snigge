If someone has invited you to a Zoom meeting, you may be wondering what
to click, how to turn on your camera, or how to make sure people can
hear you. Zoom can look busy the first time you open it, but you only
need a few basic controls to take part.

Zoom is a video meeting service that lets people talk over the internet
using a computer, tablet, or phone. You can join most meetings without
creating a Zoom account, and you do not need a camera if you would
rather take part using audio only.

## What you'll need

-   A computer, tablet, or smartphone connected to the internet.
-   A microphone and speakers or headphones if you want to speak and
    listen.
-   A webcam or built-in camera if you want other people to see you.
-   The meeting invitation link or meeting ID and passcode, if one was
    provided.

## Step 1: Open Zoom or join from an invitation

You can join a meeting by clicking its invitation link or by entering
its meeting ID in Zoom. The link is usually sent by email, calendar
invitation, or message.

1.  Open the meeting invitation.
2.  Click the **Join Zoom Meeting** link, if one is provided.
3.  If your device asks whether to open Zoom, choose the option to open
    the Zoom app.
4.  If you do not have the app, you may be offered a way to join through
    your web browser. Follow the on-screen instructions.
5.  If asked, enter the meeting passcode from the invitation.
6.  Enter your name if Zoom asks how you would like to appear to other
    participants.

You can also open the Zoom app and select **Join**, then enter the
meeting ID and your name. A meeting ID is a number that identifies the
meeting. The host may also require a passcode.

Join button\]

> **Good to know:** You do not usually need a Zoom account just to join
> someone else's meeting. The host may have settings that require you to
> sign in, so follow the instructions in the invitation if that happens.

## Step 2: Check your microphone and camera

Before you enter the meeting, Zoom may show a preview screen. This gives
you a chance to check your camera and choose whether to join with video.

1.  If you see a video preview, check that the picture looks as you
    expect.
2.  Choose **Join with Video** if you want others to see you, or **Join
    without Video** if you prefer to keep your camera off.
3.  If Zoom asks about audio, choose **Join with Computer Audio** or the
    equivalent option to use your device's microphone and speakers.
4.  If you are using headphones, make sure they are connected before
    joining.
5.  If prompted, allow Zoom to use your microphone and camera.

You can take part without a webcam. You can also keep your microphone
muted until you are ready to speak.

\[SCREENSHOT: Zoom pre-meeting video preview with Join with Video and
Join without Video choices\]

> **Tip:** If you are joining from a shared room, check what your camera
> will show before turning it on. You can keep video off and still
> listen and speak.

## Step 3: Learn the main meeting controls

Once you are in the meeting, Zoom displays a toolbar with buttons for
the most common actions. On a computer, the toolbar is usually along the
bottom of the meeting window. On a phone or tablet, the controls may be
arranged differently or hidden until you tap the screen.

1.  Select **Mute** to turn off your microphone. Select **Unmute** when
    you want to speak.
2.  Select **Stop Video** to turn off your camera. Select **Start
    Video** to turn it on again.
3.  Select **Participants** to see who is in the meeting, if that option
    is available.
4.  Select **Chat** to send a written message to the host or other
    participants, depending on the meeting settings.
5.  Select **Leave** when you want to exit the meeting.

The host may control some options. For example, a host may mute
participants, turn off chat, or prevent participants from sharing their
screens.

\[SCREENSHOT: Zoom meeting toolbar with Mute, Start Video, Participants,
Chat, and Leave clearly labelled\]

> **Careful:** When you are not speaking, muting your microphone can
> reduce background noise. Remember that unmuting lets everyone in the
> meeting hear sounds picked up by your microphone.

## Step 4: Use Chat to send a message

Chat is useful if you want to share a short comment, ask a question
without interrupting, or send a web address.

1.  Select **Chat** on the meeting toolbar.
2.  A chat panel should open.
3.  Type your message in the message box.
4.  Check who will receive it. Depending on the host's settings, you may
    be able to send a message to everyone or to a specific participant.
5.  Press **Enter** or select the send icon.

Not every meeting allows private messages or file sharing. If you cannot
send a message, the host may have disabled that feature.

\[SCREENSHOT: Zoom Chat panel showing the message box and recipient
selector\]

## Step 5: Share your screen when you need to show something

Screen sharing lets other people see content from your computer or
device. You might use it to show a document, a photograph, or a
presentation.

1.  Select **Share** or **Share Screen** on the meeting toolbar.
2.  Choose what you want to share, such as a particular application
    window or your entire screen.
3.  Check that you have selected the correct window or screen.
4.  Select **Share** to begin.
5.  When you have finished, select **Stop Share**.

If you are showing a document, sharing just that app window can help
keep other things on your desktop private. Close or hide anything you do
not want participants to see before you begin.

\[SCREENSHOT: Zoom screen-sharing selection window showing options for a
screen or application window\]

> **Tip:** If you are new to screen sharing, practise with a trusted
> person before an important meeting. It is easier to learn the controls
> when you are not in a hurry.

## Step 6: Schedule your own Zoom meeting

You can host a meeting by starting one straight away or scheduling it
for later. To schedule a meeting in the Zoom desktop app, you generally
need to sign in to your Zoom account.

1.  Open the Zoom app and sign in.
2.  Go to the **Home** tab.
3.  Select **Schedule**.
4.  Enter a meeting topic, date, and time.
5.  Review the available security, video, and audio options.
6.  Select **Save**.
7.  Copy the invitation details or use the available calendar invitation
    option to send them to the people you want to invite.

The layout can differ between the desktop app, mobile app, and web
portal. If you do not see **Schedule** on the Home tab, check the
**Calendar** or **Meetings** area, or use the scheduling option in your
Zoom account online.

\[SCREENSHOT: Zoom Schedule meeting window showing the topic, date,
time, and Save button\]

For more details, see Zoom's official guide to [scheduling
meetings](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0060700).

## Step 7: Leave the meeting safely

When the conversation is over, you can leave without shutting down your
computer or closing all your apps.

1.  Find **Leave** on the meeting toolbar.
2.  Select **Leave Meeting** if a confirmation menu appears.
3.  Wait until the meeting window closes.
4.  If you are the host, Zoom may show an option to end the meeting for
    everyone instead. Choose carefully.

If you are a participant, leaving the meeting does not end it for
everyone else. The host can continue the meeting after you go.

## Troubleshooting: Common Zoom problems

**People cannot hear me.**

Check that you are not muted. Select the small arrow beside the
microphone control, if shown, and make sure Zoom is using the correct
microphone. Also check that your device has allowed Zoom to use the
microphone.

**I cannot hear anyone else.**

Turn up your device's volume and check that Zoom is using the right
speakers or headphones. If you use Bluetooth headphones, make sure they
are connected to the device.

**My camera is not working.**

Check that the camera is not covered and that **Start Video** is
selected. Close other apps that may be using the camera, and check your
computer or phone's privacy settings if Zoom does not have camera
permission.

**The meeting link does not open.**

Try opening the Zoom app first and selecting **Join**, then enter the
meeting ID from the invitation. You can also ask the host to send the
invitation again.

**The controls have disappeared.**

Move your mouse over the meeting window on a computer, or tap the screen
on a phone or tablet. The toolbar may hide itself when it is not being
used.

For more help, visit Zoom's [official support
center](https://support.zoom.com/).

## Feel more comfortable using Zoom

You do not need to master every Zoom feature to enjoy a meeting. Start
by joining with the invitation link, checking your audio, and learning
how to mute and unmute. Once those basics feel familiar, you can explore
Chat, screen sharing, and scheduling at your own pace.
