If your Samsung phone's screen feels crowded or the text and icons are difficult to see, **Samsung Easy Mode** can make everyday use more comfortable. It gives the Home screen a simpler layout, with larger text and icons and a more straightforward way to find the apps you use most.

Easy Mode is built into many Samsung Galaxy phones, so you usually don't need to download anything. The steps below cover recent Samsung software, although the wording and layout can vary slightly by model and version.

## Step 1: Open your Samsung display settings

Easy Mode is a display option in the Settings app.

1. Unlock your Samsung phone.
2. Find and tap **Settings**. The icon usually looks like a gear.
3. Scroll through the list and tap **Display**.
4. Look for **Easy mode**.

[SCREENSHOT: Samsung Settings menu with Display highlighted]

If you don't see Easy mode straight away, use the search icon or search field at the top of **Settings**. Type “Easy mode” and select the matching result if it appears.

> **Good to know:** Easy Mode is a Samsung feature. Other Android phone brands may offer different accessibility or display options, but they may not use the same name or layout.

## Step 2: Turn on Easy Mode

Once you have opened the Easy Mode settings, you can switch it on.

1. Tap **Easy mode**.
2. Review the description on the screen so you know what will change.
3. Turn on the **Easy mode** switch.
4. Wait a moment while your phone changes the Home screen layout.

[SCREENSHOT: Samsung Easy mode settings page showing the Easy mode switch]

Your phone may briefly refresh the screen. This is normal. When it finishes, the Home screen should look simpler, with larger icons and text and more space between items.

> **Tip:** Give yourself a little time to get used to the new layout. Your phone's main functions are still there, but some items may be in different places.

## Step 3: Adjust the touch-and-hold delay if needed

Easy Mode may include a setting called **Touch and hold delay**. This controls how long you need to press an item before the phone treats it as a long press.

1. Stay in the **Easy mode** settings.
2. Look for **Touch and hold delay**.
3. Tap it if you want to adjust the timing.
4. Choose a delay that feels comfortable.

[SCREENSHOT: Easy Mode settings showing the Touch and hold delay option]

A longer delay can help if your phone often responds to a touch when you meant to tap or scroll. The best setting is the one that feels natural when you use the screen.

## Step 4: Add your most-used contacts to the Home screen

Some Samsung Easy Mode versions let you add contacts to a dedicated Home screen page. This can make it quicker to call or message people you contact often.

1. From the Home screen, swipe to the contacts page if one is shown.
2. Tap the option to add a contact, if available.
3. Choose a person from your contacts.
4. Repeat for other people you want to reach easily.

[SCREENSHOT: Samsung Easy Mode Home screen showing the contacts page and add-contact option]

The contacts page and its controls can differ between Samsung models and software versions. If you do not see this option, you can still use the **Phone** or **Contacts** app as usual.

> **Careful:** Check that you select the right person when adding a contact. If two people have similar names, look at the phone number or other details before choosing.

## Step 5: Make text and display items more comfortable

Easy Mode enlarges parts of the interface, but you can also adjust other display settings to suit your needs.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Display**.
3. Tap **Font size and style**.
4. Move the font-size slider until the text is comfortable to read.
5. Return to **Display** and look for **Screen zoom** if you want menus and other screen items to appear larger.

[SCREENSHOT: Samsung Display settings showing Font size and style and Screen zoom]

You can change these settings again at any time. If the text becomes so large that some words are cut off, move the slider back a little.

## Step 6: Turn off Easy Mode if you prefer the standard layout

You can return to the usual Samsung Home screen whenever you want. Switching Easy Mode off does not delete your photos, contacts, messages, or apps.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Display**.
3. Tap **Easy mode**.
4. Turn off the **Easy mode** switch.
5. Wait for the standard Home screen layout to return.

[SCREENSHOT: Samsung Easy mode settings with the switch turned off]

Your phone will return to its regular layout. Any separate font-size or screen-zoom adjustments may remain, so check those settings too if the display still looks larger than before.

## Troubleshooting: If you can't find Easy Mode

If the instructions do not match your Samsung phone, try these suggestions:

- **Easy Mode is missing:** Open **Settings** and search for “Easy mode.” Availability and menu placement can vary by model and software version.
- **The screen did not change:** Check that the Easy Mode switch is on, then return to the Home screen. If needed, restart the phone and check again.
- **Text is still too small:** Go to **Settings** > **Display** > **Font size and style** and increase the font size.
- **Some icons or apps look different:** Easy Mode changes the Home screen layout, but individual apps may keep their own designs and text sizes.
- **You have a different Samsung layout:** Install available software updates from **Settings** > **Software update** if appropriate, then search Settings again for “Easy mode.”

## Enjoy a simpler Samsung screen

Easy Mode can make a Samsung phone feel less cluttered by giving you larger text and icons and a simpler Home screen. Try it for a few days, adjust the display to suit you, and remember that you can switch back whenever you like.
