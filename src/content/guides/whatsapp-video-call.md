WhatsApp lets you make **free video calls** to anyone who also has WhatsApp, anywhere in the world. All you need is an internet connection. Here's how.

## Before you start

- You and the other person both need **WhatsApp** installed.
- The other person needs to be **saved in your contacts**.
- A **Wi-Fi connection** is best. Video calls use a lot of mobile data.

## Step 1: Open the chat

1. Open **WhatsApp** (the green icon with a white phone).
2. Tap **Chats** at the bottom (on iPhone) or at the top (on Android).
3. Tap the name of the person you want to call.

> **Tip:** If you've never chatted with them, tap the **new chat** button (a pencil or speech bubble with a "+") and choose their name.

## Step 2: Start the video call

At the top of the chat, you'll see two icons:

- A **video camera** icon starts a **video call**.
- A **phone** icon starts a **voice call** (no video).

Tap the **video camera**. WhatsApp will ring the other person.

> **First time?** Your phone may ask for permission to use the camera and microphone. Tap **Allow** or **OK**.

## Step 3: During the call

Tap the screen once to show the buttons at the bottom:

- **Microphone**: mutes you so they can't hear you. Tap again to unmute.
- **Camera**: turns your video off or on.
- **Flip camera** (two arrows): switches between your front and back camera, which is handy for showing the grandkids your garden.
- **Red button**: ends the call.

> **Tip:** Hold your phone at eye level and sit facing a window or lamp. The other person will see you much better.

## How to answer a video call

When someone video calls you, your screen will show their name.

- **On iPhone:** tap **Accept** (or swipe the button if your phone is locked).
- **On Android:** swipe up on the green button or tap **Answer**.

## How to make a group video call

You can video call up to **32 people** at once.

1. Open a **group chat** you're already in.
2. Tap the **video camera** icon at the top.
3. Choose who to call and tap **Call**.

Everyone in the group will get a ring and can join.

## It works on computers too

WhatsApp also has an app for Windows and Mac. Video calls work the same way, and the bigger screen can be easier to see.

## Enjoy your call!

Video calls are a wonderful way to stay close to family and friends. The more you use it, the easier it gets.
