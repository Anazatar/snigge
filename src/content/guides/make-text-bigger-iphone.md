If the words on your iPhone feel tiny, you're not alone, and you don't need new glasses (or a new phone). Apple has built-in settings that make everything easier to read. Let's go through them one at a time.

## Step 1: Open Settings

Find the **Settings** app on your Home Screen. It's the gray icon that looks like a set of gears. Tap it once.

## Step 2: Go to Display & Brightness

Scroll down a little and tap **Display & Brightness**.

## Step 3: Change the text size

Tap **Text Size**. You'll see a slider at the bottom of the screen.

- Drag the slider to the **right** to make text bigger.
- Drag it to the **left** to make it smaller.

The sample text at the top changes as you move the slider, so you can see the size before you leave.

> **Tip:** Most apps that come with your iPhone, like Mail, Messages and Notes, will follow this setting automatically.

## Step 4: Make the text bold (optional)

Go back to **Display & Brightness** and turn on **Bold Text**. The switch turns green when it's on. Bold text is often easier to read than bigger text alone.

## Need it even bigger?

The slider in Step 3 has a limit. To go past it:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Accessibility**.
3. Tap **Display & Text Size**.
4. Tap **Larger Text**.
5. Turn on **Larger Accessibility Sizes**, then drag the slider to the right.

## Make everything bigger, not just text

**Display Zoom** makes icons, buttons and text larger all at once.

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Display & Brightness**.
2. Tap **Display Zoom**.
3. Choose **Larger Text** (on some models it's called **Zoomed**).
4. Tap **Done**. Your iPhone will restart the screen to apply the change.

> **Good to know:** Menu names can change slightly with each iOS update. If you can't find something, open Settings and use the search bar at the top. Type "text size" and it will take you straight there.

## That's it!

You've made your iPhone easier on the eyes. These settings work the same way on an iPad, too.
