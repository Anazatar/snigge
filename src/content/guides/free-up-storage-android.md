When your Android phone runs out of space, it gets slow, apps stop updating and the camera may refuse to take pictures. The good news: you can usually free up a lot of room in a few minutes, without deleting anything you care about.

> **Before you start:** Android phones are made by many companies (Samsung, Google, Motorola and others), so menu names can be a little different on your phone. If you can't find something, open **Settings** and tap the magnifying glass to search.

## Step 1: See what's taking up space

1. Open **Settings** (the gear icon).
2. Tap **Storage**.
   - On **Samsung** phones, tap **Battery and device care**, then **Storage**.

You'll see a bar showing what uses the most space: usually photos, videos and apps.

## Step 2: Use the Files app to clean up

Most Android phones have Google's **Files** app (a blue folder icon). If you don't have it, you can download "Files by Google" free from the Play Store.

1. Open **Files**.
2. Tap **Clean** at the bottom.
3. You'll see suggestions like **Junk files**, **Duplicate files** and **Large files**.
4. Start with **Junk files**. They are safe to delete. Tap **Clean** and confirm.

> **Tip:** For duplicate and large files, look at each one before you delete it. Old downloaded videos are often the biggest space-wasters.

## Step 3: Let Google Photos remove backed-up pictures

If you use **Google Photos** and your pictures are backed up, you can delete the copies stored on your phone. They stay safe in your Google account.

1. Open **Google Photos**.
2. Tap your **profile picture** in the top corner.
3. Look for **Free up space** and tap it.
4. Google Photos will tell you how much space you'll get back. Tap **Free up** to confirm.

Your photos will still appear in the Google Photos app. They're just not taking up room on the phone anymore.

## Step 4: Remove apps you don't use

1. Open **Settings**, then tap **Apps**.
2. Tap an app you no longer use.
3. Tap **Uninstall**, then **OK**.

Games are often the biggest apps. If you haven't opened one in months, it's probably safe to let it go. You can always download it again later.

## Step 5: Clear the cache of big apps

Apps like WhatsApp, Facebook and Chrome store temporary files called "cache." Clearing it is safe and won't delete your messages or account.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Apps**.
2. Tap the app (for example, **Chrome**).
3. Tap **Storage**.
4. Tap **Clear cache**.

> **Careful:** Don't tap **Clear data** or **Clear storage**. That one can log you out and erase the app's saved information.

## All done!

Check **Settings → Storage** again. You should see more free space. Repeating Steps 2 and 5 once a month keeps your phone running smoothly.
